# Optimize drafts for useful shares

Apply this check to every new or revised X and LinkedIn draft. The author owns
publication. Keep one live writer per draft, preserve the author's latest edits, and
freeze all writes when a draft becomes published. Keep account analytics,
identity research, raw readbacks and review records private.

## Give a reader something worth forwarding

Name the reader and the decision the post helps them make. Put the concrete
payoff, supporting fact or useful test in the opening. For performance advice,
put the main cost or constraint there too. Compare with the actual baseline
and distinguish timed work from excluded work. Do not count shared work as
new overhead, invent an accuracy loss, or replace a tradeoff with untested cases.

Retain one useful rule, comparison, diagnostic or counterexample that a reader
can send to a colleague. Record why they would share it, what they would save,
what extra value the link provides, and the main reason they might skip it.
Ask a specific question about the reader's decision or experience when that
would improve the discussion. Avoid generic requests for likes or reposts.
These are editorial hypotheses to measure, not promises of viral reach.

Use the fewest words and posts needed without dropping material conditions,
units, source credit or evidence limits. Preserve post boundaries and media
unless the current owner explicitly includes them in the edit. Privately
record whether a reader would click, save, share or reply and what still makes
the post hard to use.

If a review invalidates a gain, method or condition, remove the unsupported
claim from the draft. Keep supported architecture or diagnostic advice and
the actual source credit. Flag affected figures and alternate copy to their
owner. A stronger hook must not amplify evidence that failed review.

Lead technical visuals with clear data, exact comparisons and the practical
tradeoff. Follow the current editorial creative direction. Gamma is optional
when it improves explanation. Neither a decorative theme nor the former
Whiteboard style is a required default.

## Credit people for a concrete contribution

Start with the author, maintainer or person whose statement or question the
post actually uses. Search their name with the employer and topic, then check
personal sites, GitHub and official author or speaker pages for cross-links.
Verify X and LinkedIn separately and retain the searches and ambiguities.
Do not infer a handle or person URN, tag a famous person by association, or
substitute an employer tag for an unresolved person.

Keep credit close to the statement or result it supports. Lead an original X
post with reader value before attribution. In an actual reply, preserve the
exact native parent and recipient. A source reply is not a standalone growth
post, and should answer that conversation first.

Verify saved mention placement separately from profile identity. A plain name
is not a native LinkedIn mention. Recheck Typefully's current support and use
the intended native profile only where the editor supports it. Record a known
profile with unsupported placement as `NATIVE_MENTION_PENDING` and retain the
exact limitation. Never connect another person's account or invent a URN.
Keep identity lookup failures distinct from platform limitations.

## Make the Auto-Plug continue the useful idea

Follow [AUTOPLUG_AND_RESULTS.md](AUTOPLUG_AND_RESULTS.md). Every eligible
original X draft gets a topic-specific follow-up that answers the next useful
question or offers a concrete next step. Verify that its existing destination
delivers that value. A relevant explanation or question can be enough when a
link adds nothing. Do not repeat a generic repository pitch.

Choose the threshold from dated, deduplicated comparable originals and retain
the actual ages, counts, selection rule and uncertainty privately. Keep a
provisional setting provisional. Early readings and a small cohort do not
establish a winning hook, optimal threshold or causal lift. Replies and
unsupported platforms have an explicit not-applicable reason.

After the final copy edit, inspect the exact draft's saved plug text, trigger
and dormant state, along with Auto-Retweet. Preserve global defaults. The
Typefully editor's “Spread the word” control inserts promotional copy into
the post and is not the Auto-Plug settings control. Use the actual per-draft
settings and never activate a publish or schedule action to test them.

## Preserve and verify the saved draft

Read the latest status, text, media, targets, settings and tags immediately
before writing. Reconcile changes from the author or another owner. Preserve
comment anchors and source links. Keep X, LinkedIn and the unsent source
comment consistent in meaning without forcing identical wording.

After saving, reload and select the exact draft. Check its visible content
and native mentions, full API readback, unchanged media and targets, lifecycle
tag, exact dormant promotion settings and final `UNCHANGED` guard. Confirm
draft status and null schedule, publication and public-share fields. Keep the
actual phone preview readable. Follow [TYPEFULLY_WORKFLOW.md](TYPEFULLY_WORKFLOW.md).
Copy optimization does not replace completed technical reviews or package
checks, and does not certify publication readiness.

Wait for the exact draft and its settings to finish loading before interpreting
controls. Initial unchecked controls or a cached preview can be stale. Verify
saved server copy and the reloaded editor. Typefully preview engagement counts
are placeholders, not account analytics. Edit verified text paragraphs rather
than replacing an entire rich post container that also controls media.

## Learn from actual sharing after the author publishes

Register each prepared draft in the existing private results registry. After
the author publishes, admit each exact native URL and platform publication time.
Capture 24-hour and seven-day results at their actual observation ages. Late
observations remain late. Never fabricate an earlier snapshot.

Keep X reposts, quotes, likes, replies, bookmarks, impressions and clicks
distinct. Keep LinkedIn reposts, reactions, comments, saves, sends, impressions
and link visits under their native definitions. Missing metrics are unavailable,
not zero. Do not sum a thread's views as unique reach or infer private sharing
from public reposts. Author thread continuations and first comments are not
evidence of audience responses.

Compare similar originals at similar ages and show raw counts and denominators.
Use a share rate only when metric definitions and denominators are compatible.
Track actual Auto-Plug delivery with its exact reply URL separately from its
configuration. Use the evidence to revise a future draft, not live promotion.
Existing comment monitors own response discovery and reply preparation.
Keep the results monitor quiet while nothing actionable changes.
