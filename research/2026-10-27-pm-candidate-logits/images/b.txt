Six warm-block comparisons

All six medians are milliseconds per warm block of 16 projection/filter/argmax steps. Bars show medians and whiskers show the minimum to maximum of the three paired blocks in each cell. These are three blocks in one process, not independent processes or seeds. Changing mode includes each gather; static mode reuses gathered rows after setup. Static one-time row setup is 0.034720 ms for 16 IDs, 3.252477 ms for 64 IDs, and 0.022130 ms for 256 IDs. These setup costs are outside the displayed warm-block times. The synthetic strict-FP32 stage uses batch 8, hidden width 2048, vocabulary 131072, one shared device-resident legal set, and TF32 disabled.
