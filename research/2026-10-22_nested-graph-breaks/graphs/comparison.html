Where startup time went
SVGPNGData
The extra time is in the first compiled call: True adds 786–876 ms across the four seeds. Calls 2–10 are 18.6–19.3 ms faster. The no-break seed-1 excursion is also in call 1, adding 117.6 ms while calls 2–10 are effectively unchanged. It remains a failed startup control.
The leading explanation is the cost of compiling two much larger fused programs. Recovered LLVM IR and PTX are about five times larger. Exact runtime source places CUDA Graph recording on call 2, which is faster with True. The retained run lacks compile-phase timings, so the precise split among compiler stages, loading, and initial warmup remains unmeasured. The isolated no-break cold-call excursion also has no proven phase-level cause.
