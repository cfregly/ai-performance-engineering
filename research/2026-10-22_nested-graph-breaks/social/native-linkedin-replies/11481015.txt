The original steady-state gains were 2.87× at depth 4 and 4.53× at depth 8. In a separate 4,000-call depth-8 follow-up, total wall time was ahead from call 1 in seed 0. Seed 1 crossed at call 3,007 and stayed ahead. This includes compilation and between-call time, but excludes imports, input setup, and the eager reference. With CUDA Graph replay off, neither seed broke even within 4,000 calls. These are observed crossovers, not a universal payback point.
