This is a synthetic depth-8 compiler microbenchmark with nested Python frames, one explicit graph break, and the same 64 FP32 operations. It is not a training step or a nested-loop benchmark. Backward passes, optimizers, and representative models were not tested, so the 16-to-2 region change does not justify a general default recommendation. A separate B200 test found a steady-state gain with CUDA Graph replay off, but it does not isolate the remaining causal mechanism.
