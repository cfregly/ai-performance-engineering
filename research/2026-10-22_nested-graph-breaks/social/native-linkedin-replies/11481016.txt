A separate seed-0 B200 pair measured 8 MiB of PyTorch allocated memory after 4,000 calls in both conditions. Reserved memory was 296 MiB without nested graph-break support and 168 MiB with it. First-call peak allocated memory was 170.5 MiB without support and 146.5 MiB with it. Final host RSS was 1,410.6 MiB without support and 1,416.7 MiB with it. The footprints were not identical. This does not establish a general model-memory improvement.
