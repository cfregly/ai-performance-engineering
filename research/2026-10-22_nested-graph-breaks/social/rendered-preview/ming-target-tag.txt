With nested graph-break support, Nsys shows 2 graph executions instead of 16. Internal nodes are hidden. Unprofiled repeated calls measured the speedup. Profiling adds overhead. NCU used 9 replay passes. This image shows one call after warmup.

With CUDA Graph replay off, nested graph-break support gave 3.29×/3.27× tail speedups (two seeds, last 1,000 calls), versus 4.55×/4.38× with replay on. GPU spans for profiled calls 3–5 were excluded with support. An exact CPU/GPU causal split is unproven.
