The original steady-state gains were 2.87× at depth 4 and 4.53× at depth 8, across four paired seeds. A separate depth-8 B200 follow-up tracked 4,000 calls in two paired seeds.

With nested graph-break support, total wall time was ahead from call 1 in seed 0. Seed 1 crossed at call 3,007 and stayed ahead through 4,000. This includes compilation and time between calls, but excludes imports, input setup and the eager reference. With CUDA Graph replay off, neither seed broke even within 4,000 calls despite faster steady calls. These are observed crossovers, not a universal payback threshold.
