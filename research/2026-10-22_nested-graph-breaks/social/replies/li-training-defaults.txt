This compares nested graph-break support on and off in a synthetic depth-8 workload: nested Python frames, one explicit graph break, and the same 64 FP32 operations. It is not a training step or a nested-loop benchmark. Backward passes, optimizers, and representative models were not tested, so this result alone does not justify a general default recommendation.

A separate B200 test still found a steady-state gain with CUDA Graph replay off. That does not identify the remaining causal mechanism.
