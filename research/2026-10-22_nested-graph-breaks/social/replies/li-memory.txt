A separate seed-0 B200 pair measured 8 MiB of PyTorch allocated memory after 4,000 calls, with and without nested graph-break support. Reserved memory was 296 MiB without support and 168 MiB with it. First-call peak allocated memory was 170.5 MiB without support and 146.5 MiB with it.

Final host RSS was 1,410.6 MiB without support and 1,416.7 MiB with it. The footprints were not identical. This is one pair of allocator and process measurements, not whole-device memory or a general equivalence result.
